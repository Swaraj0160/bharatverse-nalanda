# Sources

## Konark sundial geometry (Shadow & Stone, the Lens)
- 24 wheels, each with 8 wider (major) and 8 thinner (minor) spokes; ~9'9" dia.
- 8 major spokes divide 24 h into 8 parts of 3 h; 8 minor spokes at the exact
  midpoints give a mark every 1 h 30 m.
- ~30 beads on the rim between a minor and a major spoke → 3 min per bead;
  beads elongated so a shadow can fall left/centre/right → ~1 min resolution.
- Read anticlockwise; the top-centre major spoke is 12 midnight; a finger/rod at
  the axle casts the reading shadow.
- Basis: published surveys and popular-science accounts of the Konark wheels'
  use as sundials; UNESCO World Heritage inscription (Sun Temple, Konârak,
  1984); ASI site records. Encoded once in `lib/konark.ts`; every wheel drawing
  derives from it.

## Rebuild India (Konark deul profile)
- Kalinga (Odishan) temple form divided into pitha (platform), bada (wall),
  gandi (curvilinear tower), and mastaka (crowning members: beki, amalaka,
  kalasha). The tower steps inward so its mass stays over the wall below.
- Basis: standard published descriptions of Odishan temple architecture; ASI
  Konark site records. Simplified to a teachable six-course stack.
