# Credits & provenance

## 3D and 2D geometry
Every model in BharatVerse — the Konark chariot wheel (Lens and game), the
temple-profile silhouette in Rebuild India, the papercraft marker — is authored
procedurally in code from published measurements. **No 3D mesh, texture, HDRI,
or scan is downloaded or bundled.** There is therefore no CC-BY attribution debt.

Parametric source for the wheel: 8 major spokes (3 h apart), 8 minor spokes at
the midpoints (1.5 h), a beaded rim (30 beads per half-sector = 3 min each),
read anticlockwise from midnight at the top. See `lib/konark.ts` and
`docs/SOURCES.md`.

## Historical content
All passages are written in plain words from general historical knowledge and
carry a plain-language source label describing the *kind* of record a claim
rests on. No page/ISBN citations are fabricated; disputed points (e.g. the fall
of the Konark deul) are marked as disputed. Sites referenced: Konark Sun Temple,
Nalanda Mahavihara, Rani ki Vav — all UNESCO World Heritage Sites and
ASI-protected. No government partnership or endorsement is claimed.

## AI
Bharat Guide facts are retrieved from the curated corpus; phrasing may be
generated (Gemini, optional) but never facts. Unverifiable questions are
refused.
